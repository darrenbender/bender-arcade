Bender Robo Mobs

A Minecraft add-on: ride 12 giant robot mobs, each with its own special attack, and battle your brother in Battle Mode!

⬇️ Download for Minecraft

For Minecraft Bedrock on iPad, iPhone, Android and Windows. Free, no ads.

How to put it on the iPad

  1. Tap Download for Minecraft above, then tap Download when Safari asks.
  2. Open the Files app, go to Downloads, and tap BenderRoboMobs.mcaddon. Minecraft opens and says Successfully imported.
    If tapping the file doesn't open Minecraft, tap the Share button and choose Minecraft.
  3. In Minecraft, tap Create New World (or the pencil next to an old world). Scroll to Behavior Packs, tap Available, tap Bender Robo Mobs, then Activate. It works alongside the other Bender add-ons.
  4. Tap Create or Play. The first time you join, you pick your first robot and get a Robo Remote.

How to ride

  1. Tap the ground with a robot kit to put the robot down, then tap the robot to climb on. Sneak to get off.
  2. Walking robots move like a horse. Hold jump to jump high.
  3. Flying robots (Blaze, Ghast, Wither): look where you want to go and push forward. Hold jump to climb. Look down to land. The Robo Guardian zooms underwater the same way.
  4. Hold the Robo Remote and tap to use your robot's special attack. Hit monsters for a robo punch.
  5. To pack a robot away, hit it 3 times when nobody is riding it. You get the kit back.

⚔️ Battle Mode

  • Get off your robot and tap the Robo Remote, then switch Battle Mode on. Both pilots have to switch it on.
  • Then your attacks and punches hit the other robot's armor (the bar at the bottom of the screen).
  • When a robot's armor runs out, it's knocked out: the pilot pops out safely and the robot repairs itself in 10 seconds.
  • Players never get hurt. The remote menu also shows how many knockouts everyone has.

Good to know

  • Your robot's armor protects you while you ride, so monsters hurt you a lot less.
  • The Robo Spider climbs walls: just walk straight into one. The Robo Slime smashes the ground when it lands from up high.
  • A flying robot you leave in the air floats gently down to the ground.
  • Each special attack takes a few seconds to recharge. The bar at the bottom shows when it's ready.
  • Worlds with add-ons don't earn Xbox achievements.
