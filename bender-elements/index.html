Bender Elements

A Minecraft add-on: master ten elements with magic staffs, beat the element guardians, and wear their armor!

⬇️ Download for Minecraft

For Minecraft Bedrock on iPad, iPhone, Android and Windows. Free, no ads.

How to put it on the iPad

  1. Tap Download for Minecraft above, then tap Download when Safari asks.
  2. Open the Files app, go to Downloads, and tap BenderElements.mcaddon. Minecraft opens and says Successfully imported.
    If tapping the file doesn't open Minecraft, tap the Share button and choose Minecraft.
  3. In Minecraft, tap Create New World (or the pencil next to an old world). Scroll to Behavior Packs, tap Available, tap Bender Elements, then Activate. It works alongside the other Bender add-ons.
  4. Tap Create or Play. The first time you join, you choose your element and get its staff, plus 2 Guardian Orbs.

How to bend

  1. Hold your staff and tap Use Power! (or tap the screen) to use the power that's chosen. The bar at the bottom shows which one.
  2. Sneak and tap to switch to the next power. Each staff has 4.
  3. Powers use energy, which fills back up by itself in a few seconds.
  4. The 4th power is your ultimate. It charges up slowly, and much faster when your powers hit monsters. When it says ULTIMATE READY!, let it rip!

The guardians

  1. Hold a Guardian Orb and tap the ground. The guardian rises out of a whirl of sparkles!
  2. Each guardian fights with its own element's powers, and uses its ultimate when it gets hurt.
  3. Beat it and everyone nearby wins that element's staff and 6 shards. Make more orbs to fight again and collect more shards.

Element armor

Craft armor from a guardian's shards, the same way you'd make iron armor. Wear all 4 pieces of one element for a bonus, and your energy refills faster with that element's staff.

Good to know

  • Powers only hurt monsters and guardians. They never hurt you, your friends, pets or animals.
  • Walls and spikes made by powers disappear by themselves after a few seconds.
  • If nobody fights a guardian for a minute, it goes back into its orb and you get the orb back.
  • In Creative mode, every staff, orb and armor piece is in the inventory, so you can try them all.
  • Worlds with add-ons don't earn Xbox achievements.
