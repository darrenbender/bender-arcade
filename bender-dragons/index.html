Bender Dragons

A Minecraft add-on: fight the Ender Dragon right in the Overworld, then hatch, raise and ride a dragon of your very own!

⬇️ Download for Minecraft

For Minecraft Bedrock on iPad, iPhone, Android and Windows. Free, no ads.

How to put it on the iPad

  1. Tap Download for Minecraft above, then tap Download when Safari asks.
  2. Open the Files app, go to Downloads, and tap BenderDragons.mcaddon. Minecraft opens and says Successfully imported.
    If tapping the file doesn't open Minecraft, tap the Share button and choose Minecraft.
  3. In Minecraft, tap Create New World (or the pencil next to an old world). Scroll to Behavior Packs, tap Available, tap Bender Dragons, then Activate. It works alongside the other Bender add-ons.
  4. Tap Create or Play. The first time you join you get a Dragon Arena Kit and a Dragon Whistle.

The big fight

  1. Find a big open space. Hold the Dragon Arena Kit and tap Build Arena! Sparkles show where it goes. Tap again to build it.
  2. An End island appears, with six obsidian pillars and an altar in the middle. Tap the altar and pick Easy, Normal or Hard.
  3. The Ender Dragon swoops in! First break the pink End crystals on top of the pillars, because they heal the dragon. Climb the ladders and hit them, or shoot them with a bow.
  4. Watch out for purple fireballs, and run when it dives at you! When the dragon lands on the altar, hit it as hard as you can.
  5. Beat it and everyone in the fight wins an Ender Dragon Egg!

Your own dragon

  1. Hold the egg and tap the ground. It wobbles, and after about 20 seconds a baby dragon hatches! Give it a name and pick its color.
  2. Your baby dragon flies after you everywhere. To help it grow, hold meat or fish and tap it. Feed it 3 times and it grows bigger. Feed it 3 more times and it's all grown up.
  3. Tap your grown-up dragon to climb on. Look where you want to go and walk forward to fly. Jump to go up. Sneak to get off (you float down safely). A friend can ride with you!
  4. Use the Dragon Whistle: tap it to call your dragon, sneak and tap to make it stay, and tap it while riding to shoot a fireball.

Good to know

  • The arena replaces everything inside the sparkles, so don't build it on top of your house! Sneak and tap the Dragon Arena Kit twice to undo your newest arena.
  • This dragon never breaks blocks, so your builds are safe.
  • Start on Easy. The tougher dragons have more health and stronger fireballs.
  • If everyone leaves the arena, the dragon gets bored and flies away. Tap the altar to call it back any time, and beat it again for more eggs.
  • Pet dragons can't get hurt. Grown-up dragons shoot fireballs at monsters near you, but never at animals or friends.
  • Worlds with add-ons don't earn Xbox achievements.
