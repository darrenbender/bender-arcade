Bender Mechs

A Minecraft add-on: pilot a giant mech, ride a motorcycle, team up with a helper bot, and build your own mech base!

⬇️ Download for Minecraft

For Minecraft Bedrock on iPad, iPhone, Android and Windows. Free, no ads.

How to put it on the iPad

  1. Tap Download for Minecraft above, then tap Download when Safari asks.
  2. Open the Files app, go to Downloads, and tap BenderMechs.mcaddon. Minecraft opens and says Successfully imported.
    If tapping the file doesn't open Minecraft, tap the Share button and choose Minecraft.
  3. In Minecraft, tap Create New World (or the pencil next to an old world). Scroll to Behavior Packs, tap Available, tap Bender Mechs, then Activate. It works alongside the other Bender add-ons.
  4. Tap Create or Play. The first time you join you get a mech, a motorcycle, a helper bot, a controller, a Base Kit and 8 sliding doors.

How to pilot the mech

  1. Hold the Titan Mech and tap the ground to put it down. Tap the mech to climb into the cockpit.
  2. Walk with the joystick. The mech stomps up one-block steps by itself.
  3. Hold Jump for a rocket jump!
  4. Hit a monster for a power punch that sends it flying.
  5. Hold the Controller and tap Go! to fire the shoulder blaster. It only hits monsters.
  6. Tap Sneak to climb out.

What's inside

Titan Mech

  • 🚀 Rocket jump
  • 👊 Power punch
  • ⚡ Shoulder blaster
  • 🛡️ Armor keeps the pilot safe

Motorcycle

  • 💨 Super fast
  • 👥 2 seats
  • 🦘 Hold Jump to hop
  • 📯 Controller = horn: beep beep!

Helper Bot

  • 🤖 Follows you around
  • ⚔️ Fights monsters (not creepers)
  • 🔧 Feed it iron ingots to repair it
  • 🏷️ Comes with a name, like Bolt or Sprocket

Sliding Doors

  • 🚪 Whoosh open when you walk up
  • 🔒 Close behind you
  • 🧱 Stack them for big hangar doors

The Base Kit

Stand on open land, hold the Base Kit and tap Build Base! Sparkles show the spot. Tap again and a whole base builds around you: a hangar with a giant sliding door, a lab, a landing pad for Bender Air planes, and a mech, a motorcycle and a helper bot waiting inside.

Paint shop

Hold the Controller, look at your mech or motorcycle, and tap Go! Pick a main color and a trim color from 16 each, or tap Surprise me!

Make more

Good to know

  • The Base Kit replaces everything inside the sparkles, so don't build a base on top of your house! Sneak and tap the Base Kit twice to undo your newest base.
  • Hit a mech or motorcycle three times to pack it up. It keeps its paint.
  • To pack up a helper bot, sneak and hit it three times. Players can't hurt bots by accident.
  • Sliding doors open for players, so mobs can't sneak in when nobody's around.
  • Worlds with add-ons don't earn Xbox achievements.
