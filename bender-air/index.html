Bender Air

A Minecraft add-on with a stunt plane, a jet and a private jet. Fly them anywhere and paint them any way you like!

⬇️ Download for Minecraft

For Minecraft Bedrock on iPad, iPhone, Android and Windows. Free, no ads.

How to put it on the iPad

  1. Tap Download for Minecraft above, then tap Download when Safari asks.
  2. Open the Files app, go to Downloads, and tap BenderAir.mcaddon. Minecraft opens and says Successfully imported.
    If tapping the file doesn't open Minecraft, tap the Share button and choose Minecraft.
  3. In Minecraft, tap Create New World (or the pencil next to an old world). Scroll to Behavior Packs, tap Available, tap Bender Air, then Activate. It works alongside the other Bender add-ons.
  4. Tap Create or Play. The first time you join you get all three planes and a Paint Brush.

How to fly

  1. Hold a plane and tap the ground to put it down. Tap the plane to climb in.
  2. Push the joystick forward to speed up. When you're going fast, look up to take off!
  3. The plane flies wherever you look. Pull the joystick back to slow down.
  4. To land, look down gently until your wheels touch the ground. The plane rolls to a stop.
  5. Tap Sneak to get out. Jump out up in the sky and a parachute opens!

The paint shop

Hold the Paint Brush, look at a plane and tap Paint! Pick from 16 body colors, 16 wing colors and 8 decals (or none): stripes, flames, lightning, stars, checkers, shark teeth, rainbow and polka dots. That's almost 7,000 different planes! Or tap Surprise me!

Good to know

  • Hit a plane three times to pack it up. It keeps its paint for next time.
  • Planes can't get hurt or break. Fly into a hill and you just bonk to a stop.
  • In the Paint Shop you can switch to cockpit view if you'd rather see from the pilot's seat.
  • Planes float, so you can land on water too.
  • Worlds with add-ons don't earn Xbox achievements.
