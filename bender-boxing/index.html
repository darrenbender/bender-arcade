Bender Boxing

A Minecraft add-on: put on your boxing gloves, punch the bags, and train your way up to 20 hearts!

⬇️ Download for Minecraft

For Minecraft Bedrock on iPad, iPhone, Android and Windows. Free, no ads.

How to put it on the iPad

  1. Tap Download for Minecraft above, then tap Download when Safari asks.
  2. Open the Files app, go to Downloads, and tap BenderBoxing.mcaddon. Minecraft opens and says Successfully imported.
    If tapping the file doesn't open Minecraft, tap the Share button and choose Minecraft.
  3. In Minecraft, tap Create New World (or the pencil next to an old world). Scroll to Behavior Packs, tap Available, tap Bender Boxing, then Activate. It works alongside the other Bender add-ons.
  4. Tap Create or Play. The first time you join you get Training Gloves, a Hay Bag and a Leather Bag.

How to train

  1. Hold a bag and tap the ground to set it up. It fits inside a house too (it's 2 blocks tall).
  2. Put on your gloves. Just hold them, or wear them in the chestplate slot so your hands are free.
  3. Punch the bag! Every punch fills up your training bar. Keep a steady rhythm for a combo: combos train you faster.
  4. Fill the bar to level up and get +2 hearts. Your extra hearts stay forever, even after you die or leave the world.

Good to know

  • Hold your gloves to see your level, your hearts, and which bag to train on next.
  • If a bag says Too easy!, you've learned all it can teach you. Move up to a tougher bag.
  • To pack a bag away, crouch and punch it 3 times. You get the bag back.
  • Gloves give your punches extra power against monsters, but never hurt pets, animals or other players.
  • Drinking milk takes your extra hearts away for a second, then they come right back.
  • Worlds with add-ons don't earn Xbox achievements.
