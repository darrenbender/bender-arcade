Royal Castle

A Minecraft add-on: tap the Castle Kit and a whole castle builds itself. Then sit on your throne and rule your kingdom!

⬇️ Download for Minecraft

For Minecraft Bedrock on iPad, iPhone, Android and Windows. Free, no ads.

How to put it on the iPad

  1. Tap Download for Minecraft above, then tap Download when Safari asks.
  2. Open the Files app, go to Downloads, and tap RoyalCastle.mcaddon. Minecraft opens and says Successfully imported.
    If tapping the file doesn't open Minecraft, tap the Share button and choose Minecraft.
  3. In Minecraft, tap Create New World (or the pencil next to an old world). Scroll to Behavior Packs, tap Available, tap Royal Castle, then Activate. It works alongside the other Bender add-ons.
  4. Tap Create or Play. The first time you join you get a Castle Kit and a Royal Crown.

How to build your castle

  1. Find a big open space. Flat and dry is best.
  2. Stand in the middle, hold the Castle Kit and tap Build Castle! Green sparkles show how big it will be.
  3. Tap Build Castle! again and watch your castle rise from the ground!
  4. Put on the Royal Crown, walk into the great hall, and tap the throne to sit on it. Long live the king (or queen)!

Royal treasures

Royal Crown

Wear it on your head like a helmet. Sit on the throne while you're wearing it for a sparkly royal welcome!

Portcullis

An iron castle gate that rises when you walk up and drops when you leave. Craft more for your own builds.

Good to know

  • The Castle Kit replaces everything inside the sparkles, so don't build a castle on top of your house! Sneak and tap the Castle Kit twice to undo your newest castle.
  • Look for a ladder going down in the great hall floor. It leads to the treasure vault.
  • There are saddles in the vault for the horses in the stables.
  • Climb any tower's ladder to reach the wall walk and the rooftops.
  • Worlds with add-ons don't earn Xbox achievements.
