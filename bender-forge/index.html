Bender Forge

A Minecraft add-on with 10 weapons and tools that have super powers

⬇️ Download for Minecraft

For Minecraft Bedrock on iPad, iPhone, Android and Windows. Free, no ads.

How to put it on the iPad

  1. Tap Download for Minecraft above, then tap Download when Safari asks.
  2. Open the Files app, go to Downloads, and tap BenderForge.mcaddon. Minecraft opens and says Successfully imported.
    If tapping the file doesn't open Minecraft, tap the Share button and choose Minecraft.
  3. In Minecraft, tap Create New World (or tap the pencil next to an old world). Scroll down to Behavior Packs, tap Available, tap Bender Forge, then tap Activate. The textures turn on by themselves.
  4. Tap Create or Play. Open the Recipe Book in a crafting table to find all 10 new items. In Creative mode they're in the inventory with the swords and tools.

Good to know: worlds with add-ons don't earn Xbox achievements. To play with a friend on Switch or Xbox, host the world from the iPad and they can join it.
