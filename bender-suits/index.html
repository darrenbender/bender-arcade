Bender Power Suits

A Minecraft add-on: put on a power suit and fly! Eight suits, each with its own super power, plus six cool gadgets.

⬇️ Download for Minecraft

For Minecraft Bedrock on iPad, iPhone, Android and Windows. Free, no ads.

🌅 Want the world to look more real while you fly? Add Bender Realism.

How to put it on the iPad

  1. Tap Download for Minecraft above, then tap Download when Safari asks.
  2. Open the Files app, go to Downloads, and tap BenderSuits.mcaddon. Minecraft opens and says Successfully imported.
    If tapping the file doesn't open Minecraft, tap the Share button and choose Minecraft.
  3. In Minecraft, tap Create New World (or the pencil next to an old world). Scroll to Behavior Packs, tap Available, tap Bender Power Suits, then Activate. It works alongside the other Bender add-ons.
  4. Tap Create or Play. The first time you join, you choose a suit and get all 4 pieces, a Power Glove, a Grapple Hook and an Energy Shield.

How to fly

  1. Put on all 4 pieces of one suit (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots).
  2. Hold jump to rocket up into the sky.
  3. Push forward while flying to boost along. Let go of jump to glide down gently. Sneak to dive.
  4. Flying uses Suit Power (the bar at the bottom). Land to fill it back up. If it runs out, you just glide down safely.

Gadgets

Gadgets work with or without a suit. Tap to use; each one recharges by itself.

Power Glove

Tap to use your suit's special power

Craft: 5 Iron Ingot + 1 Redstone

Grapple Hook

Zip to where you aim, or pull a monster to you (recharges in 1s)

Craft: 2 Iron Ingot + 1 Lead

Nova Charge

Throw it: it bursts and blasts monsters (never blocks) (recharges in 5s)

Craft: 4 Gunpowder + 1 Amethyst Shard

Energy Shield

A bubble that pushes monsters away and protects you (recharges in 15s)

Craft: 4 Iron Ingot + 4 Glass + 1 Redstone

Phase Beacon

Tap to mark a spot, tap again to warp back to it (recharges in 1s)

Craft: 1 Ender Pearl + 3 Iron Ingot

Seismic Charge

A shockwave that throws monsters up (recharges in 6s)

Craft: 4 Cobblestone + 1 Gunpowder

Hologram Decoy

A copy of you that monsters chase instead (recharges in 15s)

Craft: 3 Glass + 1 Redstone + 1 Iron Ingot

Good to know

  • Suit powers and gadgets only hurt monsters. They never hurt you, your friends, pets or animals, and they never break blocks.
  • Take your suit off in the air? No problem: you float down safely.
  • The Hologram Decoy looks just like you, so monsters chase it instead. It fades after 15 seconds.
  • The Phase Beacon remembers one spot. Tap it to set the spot, tap again to warp back there from anywhere in the same world. Sneak and tap to move the spot.
  • In Creative mode, every suit and gadget is in the inventory.
  • Worlds with add-ons don't earn Xbox achievements.
